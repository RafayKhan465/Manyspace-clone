Privacy Policy

This app is a personal tool the owner of this Instagram account uses to reply automatically to comments and direct messages on their own account.

What data is processed

When you comment on one of the account's posts or send it a direct message, Instagram sends this app your Instagram-scoped user ID, your username and the text of your comment or message. The app uses this only to decide whether to send you an automatic reply, and to send it.

What is stored

The app keeps your Instagram-scoped ID, your username, when you interacted, and a short activity log of recent comments and messages and the replies sent. This data is never sold or shared with anyone, and is used for nothing else.

Retention

The activity log is limited to the most recent entries and older entries are deleted automatically. Technical records used to prevent duplicate replies are deleted after 8 days.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, send a direct message to the Instagram account saying "delete my data", or contact the account owner. Your data will be removed within 30 days.
